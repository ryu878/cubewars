The exact 29.7%-56.0% spread across all 9 runs is consistent with expected live-timing variance on small samples (~26-40 fire attempts per run), not evidence the defect is smaller than claimed or the fix is marginal — the before/after contrast is unambiguous in every single run.

- Traced the full `socket.on("message", ...)` handler start-to-finish (`server/src/index.ts:188-317`) to confirm the rolling-window check is truly the first thing that runs, and that `timestamps.push(now)` happens unconditionally for anything that clears the check — before the `isBinary` branch, before the size check, before `JSON.parse`, before type dispatch — so every one of the 8 message shapes in scope (join/input/fire/switch_weapon/binary/oversized/non-JSON/unknown-type) is uniformly counted.
